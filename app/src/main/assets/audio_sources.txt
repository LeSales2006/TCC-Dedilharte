Dedilharte uses only the bundled Meta Steel Guitar OGG samples.
Inventory: guitar/manifest.json (source WAV names map to ogg/*.ogg).
String, pitch and velocity map: guitar/meta-steel-guitar.sfz, channels 2-7.
Velocity layers: 1-48 / 49-96 / 97-127. Default exercise velocity: 80.
Low E fret 7 uses the SFZ A#2 recording one semitone up.
Frets 21-24 extend the nearest recording on the same string locally.
Short samples use SoundPool; long samples stream from APK assets through MediaPlayer.
If SoundPool rejects a sample or does not confirm loading within five seconds,
the same local OGG falls back to MediaPlayer. Loading never starts playback by itself.
The cache holds at most 24 samples, preparing three events ahead and preserving ringing strings.
There are no audio downloads, remote URLs or online fallbacks.
